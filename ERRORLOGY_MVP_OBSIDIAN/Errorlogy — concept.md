# Errorlogy — concept

**Errorlogy** — a frame (not a brand): studying governance errors as **observable objects**.

## Four theses

1. **Governance = management** — organs of power under uncertainty; same failure modes as any management system.
2. **Office ≠ competence** — authority to act ≠ ability to act well; the gap is observable.
3. **Errors via gaps, not outcomes** — delta recorded: *declared / known / decided*, not post-hoc outcome.
4. **Catalog over accusation** — a named mode from taxonomy is testable; moral verdict — not.
