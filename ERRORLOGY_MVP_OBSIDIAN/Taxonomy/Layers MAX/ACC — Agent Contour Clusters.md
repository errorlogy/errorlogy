# ACC - Agent Contour Clusters

Agent / Contour Contribution Clusters Layer: identifies clusters of homo-agents, strategies, institutional contours and multi-environment signals whose combined contribution to an error regime is maximal. Analytical contribution only; not legal guilt.

## Principle

Government-management systems are heterogeneous. Errors often emerge not from a single actor, but from clusters formed by fragments of multiple governance contours: agencies, legal procedures, procurement channels, expert communities, political factions, media environments, social-media dynamics, and capacity mismatches. ACC identifies where the combined contribution to error activation is maximum.

**Status in JSON:** data_analysis_and_contribution_layer

## Modes / types (10)

| ID | Name | Definition (brief) |
|----|----------|---------------------|
| `ACC-001` | Capacity-veto cluster | Low or mismatched task capacity combines with high veto or delay power. |
| `ACC-002` | Legal-delay cluster | Legal ambiguity, overconstraint, jurisdictional conflict and procedural delay produce error persistence. |
| `ACC-003` | Procurement-rent cluster | Procurement, vendor networks, emergency exceptions and incentive misalignment concentrate benefits and shift externalities. |
| `ACC-004` | Narrative-amplification cluster | Media/social dynamics amplify simplified narratives, synthetic consensus or outrage equilibria. |
| `ACC-005` | Expert-exclusion cluster | Right-tail expertise is excluded or underweighted by status hierarchy, legal formalism or procedural equality. |
| `ACC-006` | Data-silo / measurement cluster | Fragmented data, KPI gaming and dashboard blindness distort representation and aggregation. |
| `ACC-007` | Interagency churn cluster | Coordination rituals multiply without operational closure, producing meeting churn and decision deferral. |
| `ACC-008` | Externality-displacement cluster | Actors keep benefits within their mandate while pushing costs to other populations, agencies or time horizons. |
| `ACC-009` | Technical-debt dependency cluster | Legacy systems, supplier dependency and integration failure accumulate until correction becomes expensive. |
| `ACC-010` | Crisis-compression cluster | Emergency pressure compresses analysis, procurement, legal safeguards and expertise routing. |
