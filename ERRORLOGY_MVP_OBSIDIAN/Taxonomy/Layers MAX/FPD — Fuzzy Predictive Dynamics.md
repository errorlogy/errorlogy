# FPD - Fuzzy Predictive Dynamics

Fuzzy Predictive Dynamics Layer: forecasts trajectories of fuzzy error memberships, weak-signal accumulation, PNO regime transitions, cluster danger and improvement counterfactuals.

## Principle

Errorlogy forecasts are not claims of certain future events. They are fuzzy trajectories of error memberships, system regimes, weak-signal accumulation and contribution-cluster danger under stated assumptions.
