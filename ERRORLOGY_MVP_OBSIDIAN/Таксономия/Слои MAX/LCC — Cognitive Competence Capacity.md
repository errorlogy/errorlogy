# LCC — Cognitive Competence Capacity

Cognitive / Competence Capacity Layer: models mismatch between task complexity and collective cognitive, professional, informational and institutional capacity of governance systems, including cognitive-capacity inequality, right-tail expertise scarcity, blocker-agents and correction capacity.

## Принцип

Government-management errors emerge when the complexity of a task exceeds the collective cognitive, professional, informational and institutional capacity of the governance system that must process it.
