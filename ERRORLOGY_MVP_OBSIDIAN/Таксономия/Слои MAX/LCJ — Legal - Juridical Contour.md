# LCJ — Legal / Juridical Contour

Legal / Juridical Contour Layer: law and legal procedures as dynamic governance contours that may constrain, hide, delay, amplify, or correct government-management errors.

## Принцип

Law is not treated as final truth. Law is modeled as a dynamic governance contour that can reduce errors, create errors, mask errors, delay decisions, or become an object of improvement.
