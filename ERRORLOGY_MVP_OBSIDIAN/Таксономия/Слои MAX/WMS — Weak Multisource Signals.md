# WMS — Weak Multisource Signals

Weak Multisource Signals Layer: detects weak signals across heterogeneous environments that are individually below action threshold but cumulatively predict error escalation.

## Принцип

Major government-management failures are often preceded by weak, distributed, heterogeneous signals. Each signal may be dismissible alone, but cross-source accumulation over time can indicate emerging CB/SF/MP/LCJ/LCC failure.

**Статус в JSON:** early_warning_and_error_accumulation_layer

## Режимы / типы (20)

| ID | Название | Определение (кратко) |
|----|----------|----------------------|
| `WMS-001` | Repeated frontline anomaly | Small repeated operational anomalies reported by frontline staff. |
| `WMS-002` | Citizen complaint micro-cluster | Localized citizen/user complaints repeat before official indicators move. |
| `WMS-003` | Expert dissent note | Minority expert warning or dissent exists but is not integrated into decision logic. |
| `WMS-004` | Audit qualification / warning | Audit or oversight body flags risk, ambiguity or lack of evidence. |
| `WMS-005` | Procurement exception pattern | Unusual vendor concentration, emergency exception use, price anomaly or qualification shortcut. |
| `WMS-006` | Deadline slippage pattern | Repeated deadline extensions or silent delays precede failure. |
| `WMS-007` | Data inconsistency signal | Different agencies/datasets disagree but mismatch is normalized. |
| `WMS-008` | Near-miss / incident precursor | Near-miss events occur but are treated as isolated. |
| `WMS-009` | Legal ambiguity accumulation | Repeated legal uncertainties or jurisdictional doubts accumulate. |
| `WMS-010` | Capacity overload indicator | Backlogs, staffing stress, call volume, error rates or queue lengths rise. |
| `WMS-011` | Briefing compression / uncertainty loss | Uncertainty and dissent disappear as information travels upward. |
| `WMS-012` | Social-media micro-narrative drift | Small online narratives begin to cluster around institutional distrust or alternative explanations. |
| `WMS-013` | Whistleblower / protected disclosure signal | A disclosure exists but is delayed, diluted or procedurally isolated. |
| `WMS-014` | Externality complaint from affected group | Affected groups report consequences missing from official rationale. |
| `WMS-015` | KPI gaming / metric distortion precursor | Metric improvement diverges from real-world quality or safety. |
| `WMS-016` | Supplier / contractor dependency warning | Dependency on a supplier, contractor, platform or private actor creates latent fragility. |
| `WMS-017` | Interagency meeting churn | Many meetings or coordination rituals occur without operational closure. |
| `WMS-018` | Budget/cost early variance | Early overruns, underestimates or optimism in cost schedule appears. |
| `WMS-019` | Technical debt / legacy system signal | Old systems, undocumented workarounds or integration failures accumulate. |
| `WMS-020` | Rights/safeguards warning | Civil rights, safety, due-process or fundamental-rights safeguards are repeatedly flagged. |
