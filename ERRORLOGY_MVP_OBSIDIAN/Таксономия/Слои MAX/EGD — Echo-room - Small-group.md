# EGD — Echo-room / Small-group

Echo-room / Small-group Dynamics Layer: models small-group group dynamics, echo rooms, hidden weak signals, informal consensus formation, dissent suppression and closed-room distortions inside government-management contours.

## Принцип

Inside government-management systems, important error-generating dynamics often happen in small groups, closed rooms, informal channels, inner circles and echo rooms. These settings can create hidden weak signals, suppress dissent, normalize anomalies and produce apparent consensus before formal procedure begins.

**Статус в JSON:** micro_group_dynamics_and_hidden_signal_layer

## Режимы / типы (12)

| ID | Название | Определение (кратко) |
|----|----------|----------------------|
| `EGD-001` | Closed-room echo reinforcement | A small decision group repeatedly validates its own assumptions in a closed communication environment. |
| `EGD-002` | Hidden dissent suppression | Dissent exists inside or near the group but is not visible in the official record or final briefing. |
| `EGD-003` | Informal micro-consensus formation | A small informal consensus forms before formal analysis, shaping what later appears as neutral procedure. |
| `EGD-004` | Status hierarchy filtering | Information is filtered according to rank, status or proximity to power rather than evidential quality. |
| `EGD-005` | Loyalty pressure distortion | Group loyalty, reputational fear or role dependence pressures agents to align with the group line. |
| `EGD-006` | Small-group risky shift | A small group adopts a riskier or more extreme position than its individual members would adopt alone. |
| `EGD-007` | Expert isolation inside group | Task-relevant expertise is present but socially or procedurally isolated from the dominant group frame. |
| `EGD-008` | Briefing convergence illusion | Briefings converge in wording and conclusion, creating the appearance of independent agreement where common-source dependence exists. |
| `EGD-009` | Informal veto norm | An agent or subgroup has no formal veto but can block change through informal status, relationships or anticipated retaliation. |
| `EGD-010` | Small-group blame avoidance loop | Agents coordinate to preserve plausible deniability or distribute responsibility before addressing the substantive error. |
| `EGD-011` | Weak-signal normalization in small groups | Repeated weak signals are normalized by a small group because acknowledging them would disrupt the shared frame or action plan. |
| `EGD-012` | Inner-circle overclosure | A small inner circle becomes the effective decision environment, reducing external correction, dissent and evidence diversity. |
