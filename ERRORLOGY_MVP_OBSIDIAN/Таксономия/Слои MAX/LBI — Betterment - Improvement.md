# LBI — Betterment / Improvement

Betterment / Improvement Layer: counterfactual and feasible alternatives for reducing error mass and improving government-management decisions.

## Принцип

Betterment means a feasible alternative that would be expected to reduce error mass, severity, or regime instability; it does not mean an ideal or hindsight-perfect solution.
