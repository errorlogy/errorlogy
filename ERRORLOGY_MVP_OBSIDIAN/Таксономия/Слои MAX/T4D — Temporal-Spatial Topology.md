# T4D — Temporal-Spatial Topology

Temporal-Spatial Topology Layer: models governance errors as trajectories in 3D+1D terrestrial topology: spatial/institutional/geographic contours plus time, including delays, windows, path dependence, temporal attractors and intervention opportunities.

## Принцип

Time is not a passive timestamp. In Errorlogy, time is a structural dimension of the topology of governance error. Earth-bound governance occurs in 3D+1D: geographic/institutional/legal/data/social contours unfold through time.

**Статус в JSON:** 4D_topology_and_time_dynamics_layer

## Режимы / типы (20)

| ID | Название | Определение (кратко) |
|----|----------|----------------------|
| `T4D-001` | Temporal topology mismatch | The time structure of the governance mechanism does not match the time structure of the problem. |
| `T4D-002` | Intervention window loss | A correctable error becomes structurally harder to correct after a missed temporal window. |
| `T4D-003` | Warning-to-action latency | The delay between weak signal detection and actual corrective action is long enough to allow error compounding. |
| `T4D-004` | Spatial-jurisdictional time lag | Different territories, agencies or jurisdictions react at different speeds, creating cross-contour temporal mismatch. |
| `T4D-005` | Temporal externality displacement | Costs are shifted into the future while current actors retain short-term benefits or avoid responsibility. |
| `T4D-006` | Policy phase desynchronization | Problem recognition, legal design, implementation, oversight and feedback operate in unsynchronized phases. |
| `T4D-007` | Slow variable blindness | Slow-moving variables accumulate outside political or dashboard attention until they dominate the outcome. |
| `T4D-008` | Fast cascade under slow governance | The problem evolves faster than legal, bureaucratic or cognitive correction cycles. |
| `T4D-009` | Delayed feedback instability | Corrective feedback arrives after the system has already moved into a different error regime. |
| `T4D-010` | Historical lock-in attractor | Past decisions create an attractor that pulls future choices toward an already suboptimal trajectory. |
| `T4D-011` | Temporal blame horizon distortion | Actors optimize for avoiding blame within their term or reporting cycle, while long-term risk rises. |
| `T4D-012` | Crisis-time compression | Emergency time pressure compresses evidence review, legal checks, procurement safeguards and dissent processing. |
| `T4D-013` | Implementation half-life decay | Policy intent decays during implementation as it passes through agencies, contractors, territories and time. |

| `T4D-016` | Temporal-scale governance mismatch | Political, legal, operational, technological and social-media time scales conflict. |
| `T4D-017` | Irreversibility threshold crossing | The system crosses a threshold after which correction becomes much more costly or impossible. |
| `T4D-018` | Temporal attractor toward PNO | The error trajectory is pulled toward a systemic PNO regime under repeated weak-signal accumulation and α-propagation. |
| `T4D-019` | Chronological evidence inversion | Later rationalizations are misread as if they were available or decisive at the earlier decision point. |
| `T4D-020` | 4D governance topology rupture | The spatial, institutional and temporal structure of the governance system breaks continuity, producing discontinuous error propagation. |
