catalog v0.1 — seed cases

politic.bar is the first applied product of errorlogy — a proposed discipline treating error as a first-class observable object. The catalog records, in a fixed schema, the gap between what a governing body claimed, what was in the public record at the time, and what it decided. Classification is against the documented taxonomy of cognitive, procedural, and informational failure modes.

Every entry carries citations, an adversarial counter-argument pass, and a neutrality audit. Every entry is versioned rather than deleted. The posture is a librarian's.
